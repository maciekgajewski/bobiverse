# Asset credits

## `ackbar.webp`

Admiral Ackbar illustration by Michael Sutfin, from The New Essential Guide to
Characters. Star Wars © Lucasfilm Ltd. Used under fair use.

- Source: <https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/File:Ackbar-NEGTC.png>
- This asset is a derivative: the original illustration was cropped to the head
  and upper body (3:4), resized to 150×200 pixels and re-encoded as WebP. It is
  shown, with the credit above as its caption, in the About ("What is this?")
  dialog (`../../docs/tasks/BOB-20261114-KA0X30-about-dialog.md`).

## `guppi-ackbar-head.webp`

Admiral Ackbar illustration by Michael Sutfin, from The New Essential Guide to
Characters. Star Wars © Lucasfilm Ltd. Used under fair use.

- Source: <https://starwars.fandom.com/wiki/File:Ackbar-NEGTC.png>
- This asset is a derivative of `ackbar.webp`: its 150×200 picture was cropped to
  the head, a 66×66 pixel square, and saved as lossless WebP. It is GUPPI's
  illustration in the inspector header, with the credit above as its alternative
  text (`../../docs/tasks/BOB-20261114-AYW3EN-info-panel-header.md`).

## `jeeves.webp`

Jeeves illustration: an original AI-assisted painting made for this project,
depicting Jeeves with the likeness of John Cleese (circa 1970, with moustache). Likeness
references were photographs of John Cleese by Alan Light (1989, CC BY 2.0) and
Bruce Baker (2014, CC BY 2.0), both on Wikimedia Commons; neither photograph is
reproduced. Painted in the style of the Admiral Ackbar illustration in
`ackbar.webp`.

- Reference: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Cleese_at_1989_Oscars.jpg>
- Reference: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:John_Cleese_(cropped).jpg>
- This asset is the full figure (3:4) cut out onto transparency, resized to
  150×200 pixels and saved as WebP. It is shown, with the credit above as its
  caption, in the What's new dialog.

## `jeeves-head.webp`

Same credit as `jeeves.webp`.

- This asset is a derivative of the same illustration: the head cropped to a
  66×66 pixel square on transparency and saved as lossless WebP. It is Jeeves's
  illustration in the inspector header, with the credit above as its
  alternative text.
